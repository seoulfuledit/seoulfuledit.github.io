Korean Perfume Hand Cream: Why Visitors Buy It

What a Korean perfume hand cream is, why visitors fill bags with them at Olive Young, and how to pick a scent, a size and a gift without guessing.

By The Seoulful Edit Editors / Published 2026-11-20
The short answer

A Korean perfume hand cream is a hand cream sold mainly on its scent, often in numbered fragrances that match a brand's perfume line. Visitors buy them as easy gifts, and Olive Young says foreign tourists made up about 40% of its hand care sales in 2026, with BEYOND, Hetras and W.DRESSROOM especially popular.

Hand cream is one of the easiest things to buy in a Korean beauty store, and visitors buy a lot of it. In an Aug. 31, 2026 press release, CJ Olive Young said foreign tourists made up about 40% of its hand care sales, and called the category a go-to travel souvenir.

The same release named the hand creams doing best with visitors: perfume hand creams from BEYOND, Hetras and W.DRESSROOM. Here is what that label means and how to pick one in the store.

What makes it a “perfume” hand cream

  • The scent leads. You choose it the way you would choose a perfume, by smell first.
  • Scents come with numbers. W.DRESSROOM numbers its scents, such as No.49 Peach Blossom, and uses the same scent names in its perfume line and its hand creams.
  • It still works as hand cream. W.DRESSROOM describes its version as a non-sticky, lightweight cream that keeps hands moist and soft.

Why visitors buy it

  • Easy gift. Olive Young itself describes hand care as a good category for travel souvenirs and gifts.
  • No skin-type guessing. You pick a scent, not a formula for someone else’s face.
  • Small and light. A tube slips into a gift bag or a coat pocket.
  • Easy to try. Smell a tester on the shelf, or ask staff for one.

How to pick one in the store

  • Test on your skin. Put a little on the back of your hand and wait a minute before you decide.
  • Match the scent to the person. Soft, clean scents are a safe pick when you don’t know someone’s taste.
  • Read the label if you’re sensitive. Fragrance is listed as an ingredient. If your skin reacts to scent, try a small area first, or choose an unscented hand cream.
  • Ask for the tax refund. Purchases of ₩15,000 or more at participating stores can qualify (as of Nov. 16, 2026. Source: VisitKorea). Bring your passport.

Who it suits

  • Good fit: gift lists, coworkers, a host, anyone who likes a signature scent.
  • Think twice: people who avoid fragrance, or friends with very sensitive skin.

Where to buy

  • In Korea: Olive Young, which named all three brands in its visitor sales release. Compare the scents on the tester shelf before you pick, and buy an extra tube if you liked one for yourself.
  • Back home: W.DRESSROOM sells its hand cream on an official US online store, in a smaller 50 ml tube.

Best for an Easy Gift: W.DRESSROOM Perfume Hand Cream

W.DRESSROOM
Perfume Hand Cream
Not sold in the US. Where to buy: Olive Young stores in Korea

Size: 100 ml (3.38 fl. oz.) in Korean stores. The brand's US store sells a 50 ml (1.69 fl. oz.) tube.

Why it's worth it: It is one of the three perfume hand cream brands Olive Young named as especially popular with visitors. The brand describes the cream as non-sticky and lightweight, made to keep hands moist and soft. Scents carry numbers, so it is easy to remember the one you liked and find it again.

Who it's for: Coworkers, a host family, a friend who likes a scent. Skip it for anyone who avoids fragrance.

How to use it: Rub a small amount into clean, dry hands. Scents include No.49 Peach Blossom, which the brand describes as the fresh scent of sweet peach juice, plus No.30 White Musk and No.97 April Cotton. Smell the tester first.

Olive Young named W.DRESSROOM among the perfume hand cream brands that are especially popular with foreign visitors (CJ Olive Young press release, Aug. 31, 2026).

More to know
  • Key ingredients: Madecassoside, ceramide, argan oil and shea butter are the key ingredients the brand's US store lists for No.49 Peach Blossom. Fragrance is on the ingredient list (checked Nov. 2026).
  • Where to buy: Olive Young stores in Korea

The three brands Olive Young named

BEYOND, Hetras and W.DRESSROOM are the perfume hand cream brands Olive Young called especially popular in its Aug. 31, 2026 release. It listed them in Korean alphabetical order and did not rank them.

  • W.DRESSROOM: the tube pictured above. It also runs an official US online store, so you can reorder at home.
  • Hetras: sells a perfume hand cream on its official Korean site.
  • BEYOND: sells hand cream sets on its official Korean site.

If the store carries all three, smell each one before you choose. The scent is the whole point.

Packing and the tax refund

  • Checked bag is simplest. Tubes are light, and you skip the carry-on liquids question. If you pack them in a carry-on, check the TSA liquids rule before you fly.
  • Keep them sealed. Tax refund items must be new, unopened and unused (as of Nov. 16, 2026. Source: VisitKorea). Open your own tube after you land.
  • Bundle small buys. A refund needs a purchase of at least ₩15,000 at a participating store (as of Nov. 16, 2026. Source: VisitKorea). Several tubes bought together go on one receipt.

Say it in Korean

  • "테스트해 봐도 돼요?" (te-seu-teu-hae bwa-do dwae-yo?) Can I try the tester?
  • "향 추천해 주세요." (hyang chu-cheon-hae ju-se-yo.) Please recommend a scent.
  • "선물 포장 돼요?" (seon-mul po-jang dwae-yo?) Can you gift wrap it?

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a Korean perfume hand cream?

It is a hand cream sold mainly on its fragrance, the way you would choose a perfume. Brands often number their scents, such as W.DRESSROOM's No.49 Peach Blossom, and use the same scent names across perfume and hand cream lines. It still works as an everyday hand cream for dry hands.

Which Korean perfume hand cream brands are popular with tourists?

Olive Young named BEYOND, Hetras and W.DRESSROOM as the perfume hand cream brands especially popular with visitors, in an Aug. 31, 2026 press release. It also said foreign tourists made up about 40% of its hand care sales. It did not rank the three brands.

Can I buy W.DRESSROOM hand cream in the US?

Yes. W.DRESSROOM runs an official US online store that sells its perfume hand cream in a 50 ml (1.69 fl. oz.) tube, checked in November 2026. Korean Olive Young stores carry the 100 ml size, so compare sizes before you buy.
