Korean Brows: How to Get the Straight, Natural Brow Look

How to do Korean brows: the straight, soft shape, brow pencil vs. brow mascara, how to fill step by step, shade words to know, and budget brow picks at Daiso.

By The Seoulful Edit Editors / Published 2026-11-24
The short answer

Korean brows are usually straighter and softer than a high, sharp arch. Fill the lower edge in a level line with a slim pencil, keep the front light and brush it upward, then set the hairs with a tinted brow mascara. Daiso stocks both brow pencils and brow mascaras, so a basic kit costs little.

Brows change how a whole face reads. The Korean brow look is a good place to start if your brows feel too sharp, too thin or too drawn on.

It is soft, fairly straight and close to your natural shape. You need two low-cost products and a few minutes.

What makes a brow look Korean

  • A straighter line. The brow stays fairly level from front to tail, with a small lift near the end. Koreans call it 일자 눈썹 (straight brows).
  • A soft front. The inner part is light and brushed up, not filled in as a solid block.
  • A gentle color. Often your hair color or a step lighter. Soft brown and taupe shades are easy to find on Korean shelves.
  • Hairs you can see. The finish looks like brushed brow hair, not a painted shape.

Brow pencil or brow mascara

  • Brow pencil: fills gaps and draws the shape. Korean brow pencils are often slim, twist-up auto pencils (오토펜슬), so you get thin strokes without a sharpener.
  • Flat or angled pencil: a wider tip for faster filling. Good if your brows are sparse.
  • Brow mascara (브로우카라): a tinted gel on a small wand. It colors the hairs and holds them in place.
  • Both: pencil for the shape, then brow mascara on top. This is an easy way to wear the look every day.

Who should buy what

  • Sparse or short brows: a slim pencil first. Add brow mascara later if you want.
  • Full brows that look heavy: a light brown brow mascara alone. It softens dark hairs.
  • Brows that won’t stay put: a brow mascara labeled fixing (픽싱) to hold them up.
  • Hot days or long walks: a pencil tagged waterproof (워터프루프).
  • First try: one slim pencil. You can add the rest once you like the shape.

Tidy before you fill

  • Brush first, then trim. Brush hairs up and trim only what sticks far above the line.
  • Shave below, not above. Remove strays under the brow to make the lower line look straight. Leave the top edge alone.
  • Go slowly. Take a little at a time and check in the mirror from arm’s length.

Make the brows match the rest

Soft brows suit the rest of the Korean makeup look: a thin cushion base and a gradient lip tint. If your hair is dyed lighter, pick a brow shade close to your new hair, not your roots. For more aisles to shop, see our Daiso beauty guide.

How to fill Korean brows, step by step

  1. Brush up. Comb the hairs up and out with a spoolie so you see the real shape.
  2. Draw the lower line. With a slim pencil, draw short strokes along the bottom edge, from below the arch to the tail. Keep it level, not curved down.
  3. Fill the gaps. Short, light strokes in the direction the hairs grow.
  4. Keep the front soft. Use what is left on the pencil, or skip it. Brush the front hairs straight up.
  5. Set it. One coat of brow mascara, brushed up at the front and along the hairs at the tail.
  6. Blend. Run a clean spoolie through once so no line looks drawn on.

Safety notes

  • Skip DIY tints. The FDA warns that permanent eyelash and eyebrow tints and dyes have been known to cause serious eye injuries, including blindness. A tinted brow mascara washes off and is not the same thing.
  • Use clean testers. The FDA says testers carry a higher risk of contamination. Swatch on your hand, or use a clean cotton swab.
  • Don't share. The FDA says not to share or swap eye cosmetics.
  • Stop if your eyes are sore. The FDA advises not using eye cosmetics if you have an eye infection or the skin around the eye is inflamed.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Korean straight brow?

It is a brow with a flat, level shape and only a small lift near the tail, instead of a high arch. Koreans call it 일자 눈썹 (straight brows). The front is soft and brushed up, and the color is often a little lighter than your hair, so the face looks soft and gentle.

Should I use a brow pencil or brow mascara?

Use a pencil to fill gaps and shape the line, and a brow mascara to tint and hold the hairs. If your brows are full, brow mascara alone may be enough. If they are sparse, start with a pencil. Many people use both: pencil first, then a light coat of brow mascara.

What brow color should I choose?

Pick a shade close to your hair color or one step lighter. Korean shelves use words like 다크브라운 (dark brown), 내추럴브라운 (natural brown), 라이트브라운 (light brown) and 그레이 (gray). Daiso Mall also tags brow products for 웜톤 (warm), 쿨톤 (cool) and 뉴트럴톤 (neutral) undertones. Swatch on your hand before you pay.
