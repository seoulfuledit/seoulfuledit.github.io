Korean Cushion Foundation: A Beginner's Guide

What a Korean cushion foundation is, how to read shades like 21N, pick dewy or matte, press it on with the puff, and save money with refills.

By The Seoulful Edit Editors / Published 2026-11-21
The short answer

A Korean cushion foundation is liquid foundation soaked into a sponge inside a compact, which you press onto skin with a puff. Pick your shade by number and undertone letter (for example 21N), choose a dewy or matte finish, and buy a set with a refill to save money.

The cushion compact is a Korean idea. Amorepacific launched the first one, the IOPE Air Cushion, in 2008, and says its cushions passed 100 million units sold worldwide by 2016.

It works like a stamp pad. A sponge soaked in liquid foundation sits in a compact, and you press it onto your face with a puff. Amorepacific says the idea came from parking-lot stamps and from customers who wanted a light lotion with UV protection and makeup in one.

Is a cushion right for you?

  • You want light, fast coverage. A cushion takes a minute and fits in a small bag.
  • You touch up during the day. The puff and mirror are built in.
  • You want full coverage. Pick a cover or matte cushion and build in thin layers.
  • You love a bare face. Try a dewy cushion and use it only where you need it.

How to read Korean shades

Korean shade codes pair a number with a letter, like 21N or 17C1. On HERA’s shade chart, shades ending in C sit under “Cool, bright rosy,” N under “Neutral” and W under “Warm, soft golden undertone.” HERA’s chart runs from 13N1 to 27N1.

  • Start with 21N if you’re unsure. Amorepacific says 21N is the most popular shade, because it subtly brightens and blends easily.
  • Swatch on your jaw, not your hand. Your face and hand are often different colors.
  • Check the range. Some lines go deeper than others. Ask staff for the deepest shade if 25 or 27 is too light.

On a budget

Daiso sells 15 g cushions for ₩5,000, like the Tag Dewy Skin Cushion, which showed 6,400+ purchases on Daiso Mall (Nov. 16, 2026). It is a cheap way to see if you like the format before you spend more. If you love it, move up to an Olive Young cushion with a refill.

One note on SPF

Many cushions list an SPF. You usually apply a thin layer of cushion, so keep your regular sunscreen underneath. Our Korean sunscreen guide covers what to pick.

How to apply a cushion with the puff

  • Prep first. Finish skincare and sunscreen, then wait a minute so the base doesn't slide.
  • Load lightly. Press the puff onto the sponge once or twice. Too much at once looks heavy.
  • Press, don't swipe. Tap the puff on your cheeks, then spread outward to your forehead, nose and chin.
  • Build where you need it. Add a second tap only on redness or spots.
  • Fold the puff for edges. Fold it in half to reach the sides of your nose and under your eyes.
  • Wash the puff. Rinse it with mild soap and let it dry fully, or keep a spare.

Dewy or matte?

Dewy (glow, 윤광): a shinier finish. Amorepacific links glow cushions to the trend for a natural, radiant complexion from 2017 to 2019.

Matte (매트) or semi-matte: less shine and more hold. Amorepacific notes that matte cushions grew in 2020 to 2022 because they didn't transfer onto face masks.

Not sure? Since 2023, Amorepacific says glow and matte have shared the top of the charts, so pick by your skin and the weather. Dry skin often likes dewy. Oily skin in a Seoul summer may prefer matte.

Refills and how to shop them

  • Look for 본품+리필. It means main product plus refill. Many Olive Young cushions are sold this way.
  • Keep the case. Amorepacific says it offers refillable cushions as standard, so you can buy refills alone later.
  • Match the brand. A refill fits only its own case. Buy the same line and shade code.
  • Pack it flat. A cushion is small and light. Keep it closed in a zip bag in case the compact pops open.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a cushion foundation?

It is a compact with a sponge soaked in liquid foundation. Amorepacific, which launched the first one in 2008, describes the core as urethane foam saturated with a mix of foundation, sunscreen and skincare. You tap the puff on the sponge and press it onto your face.

What does 21N mean in Korean foundation?

It is a shade code: a number plus an undertone letter. On HERA's own shade chart, C shades sit under "Cool, bright rosy," N under "Neutral, natural" and W under "Warm, soft golden undertone." Amorepacific says 21N is its most popular cushion shade. Brands differ, so swatch before you buy.

How long does a cushion refill last?

It depends on how much you use, so no brand gives one number. Most Korean cushions are sold with a refill in the same box, like the Olive Young best sellers in this guide. When the sponge feels dry when you press it, pop in the refill and keep the case.
