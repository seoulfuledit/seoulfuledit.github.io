Korean Skincare Routine for Oily Skin, Morning and Night

A Korean skincare routine for oily skin that keeps hydration and drops heaviness: gentle cleanser, watery toner, light lotion, sunscreen and BHA used with care.

By The Seoulful Edit Editors / Published 2026-11-23
The short answer

A Korean skincare routine for oily skin keeps the hydration and drops the heaviness: a gentle foaming cleanser, a watery toner, a light oil-free lotion or gel, and sunscreen in the morning. Dermatologists at the AAD say oily skin still needs moisturizer, and that a harsh face wash can irritate skin and trigger more oil.

Oily skin tempts you to skip moisturizer and grab the strongest face wash. The American Academy of Dermatology (AAD) says both are mistakes. A harsh cleanser can irritate your skin and trigger more oil, and oily skin still needs hydration.

Korean skincare suits oily skin because it leans on light, watery layers. You keep the hydration and leave out the heavy cream.

First, oily or combination?

Cleveland Clinic suggests a simple check. Wash and dry your face, wait about 30 minutes, then notice how it feels.

  • Oily all over: oily skin.
  • Oily on the forehead, nose and chin, dry on the cheeks: combination skin. See the split routine below.
  • Your type can change. Cleveland Clinic says season, hormones, stress and medications can all shift it, so check again when you travel.

Morning routine

  1. Gentle foaming cleanser. The AAD recommends a mild foaming face wash, up to twice a day and after sweating. Do not scrub.
  2. Watery toner (optional). A hydrating toner adds water with no oil. Pat it on with your hands.
  3. Light moisturizer. Pick a lotion or gel labeled oil free and noncomedogenic. The AAD says these labels mean the product is made not to clog pores.
  4. Sunscreen. The AAD recommends broad-spectrum SPF 30 or higher. To avoid breakouts, it suggests sunscreens with zinc oxide and titanium dioxide, without fragrance or oils. Our Korean sunscreen guide explains SPF and PA.

Night routine

  1. Take off makeup and sunscreen. The AAD says never sleep in makeup.
  2. Gentle foaming cleanser.
  3. Watery toner.
  4. BHA, 2 to 3 nights a week (optional). More on this below.
  5. Light moisturizer.

About the oil cleanser

Many Korean routines start the night with an oil cleanser. For oily skin, the AAD advises against oil-based and alcohol-based cleansers, saying they can irritate skin. If you still want to try Korean double cleansing, test it on a small area first and stop if you break out. A gentle foaming cleanser used twice is a simpler choice.

Korean product types that suit oily skin

  • Gel cream (젤 크림): a watery, light moisturizer that sits under sunscreen without a heavy layer.
  • Lotion (로션): thinner than a cream. A good daily moisturizer for oily skin.
  • BHA toner or liquid: made with salicylic acid or betaine salicylate. The AAD says salicylic acid can help reduce oiliness, but may be too harsh for some skin.
  • Blotting paper (기름종이): the AAD suggests pressing a sheet on your face for a few seconds. Do not rub, or you spread the oil.
  • Matte makeup: the AAD suggests oil-free, water-based makeup, and products labeled matte to cut shine.

Two examples from COSRX

Both are on COSRX’s official US store (checked Nov. 17, 2026).

  • BHA Blackhead Power Liquid (100 ml): COSRX lists it for oily skin and T-zone care. The ingredient list starts with willow bark water, butylene glycol, betaine salicylate and niacinamide. COSRX recommends it 2 to 3 times a week at first, then building up. It says to wear sunscreen if you use it in the daytime, not to use it with other acids such as AHAs, BHAs, retinols and vitamin C, and that sensitive skin may get irritated.
  • Oil-Free Ultra-Moisturizing Lotion with Birch Sap (100 ml): COSRX calls it a weightless lotion and lists it for oily and combination skin. Birch sap is the first ingredient, with glycerin and sodium hyaluronate for hydration. COSRX says fragrance ingredients were removed when it was reformulated. Use it after toner and before sunscreen.

Habits that help shine

  • Hands off. The AAD says touching your face spreads oil and dirt from your hands.
  • Wash after workouts. Cleveland Clinic suggests washing your face soon after exercise.
  • Warm, not hot, water. The AAD says hot water and scrubbing irritate skin, and irritated skin makes pores look more noticeable.
  • Do not pick. The AAD warns against squeezing or digging at pores.

Combination skin: split the routine

Oily only in your forehead, nose and chin? That is the T-zone, and Cleveland Clinic calls this combination skin.

  • Same cleanser, toner and sunscreen on your whole face.
  • Light lotion on the T-zone, and a little more moisturizer on dry cheeks.
  • BHA only where you need it, such as the nose, not on dry patches.

Korean words for oily skin shopping

  • 지성 (ji-seong): oily skin.
  • 복합성 (bok-hap-seong): combination skin.
  • 피지 (pi-ji): sebum, skin oil.
  • 모공 (mo-gong): pores.
  • 젤 크림 (jel keu-rim): gel cream.
  • 기름종이 (gi-reum-jong-i): blotting paper.

Show the word to a staff member if you are looking for one item.

Before you buy

  • Look for "oil free" and "noncomedogenic" on the label, the AAD suggests.
  • One acid at a time. Do not start a BHA, an AHA and a retinol in the same week.
  • Acne medicines are different. Benzoyl peroxide and prescription creams are medicines. Ask a pharmacist or a dermatologist before you add one, in Seoul or at home.
  • Patch test first. Try a new product on a small spot before using it on your whole face.

Frequently Asked Questions

Should oily skin use moisturizer?

Yes. The American Academy of Dermatology says to apply moisturizer after cleansing even if your skin is oily, to keep it hydrated. Choose one labeled oil-free and noncomedogenic. A light Korean lotion or gel cream gives water without a heavy layer.

How often should you wash oily skin?

Up to twice a day, and again after sweating, the AAD says. Use a gentle foaming face wash and do not scrub. A face wash that is too harsh can irritate your skin and trigger more oil, according to the AAD.

Can I use BHA every day on oily skin?

Start slower. COSRX, for example, recommends its BHA liquid 2 to 3 times a week at first, then building up as your skin allows. The AAD says salicylic acid can be too harsh for some skin, so use it less often or stop if your skin gets irritated.
