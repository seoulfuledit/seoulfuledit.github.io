Olive Young Must-Buys: 4 K-Beauty Picks for First-Timers

New to Olive Young? Start with four Korean skin care picks that already sell well in the US, from pore pads to sunscreen, plus tips for shopping in Seoul.

By The Seoulful Edit Editors / Published 2026-11-16 / Updated 2026-11-16
The short answer

For a first Olive Young haul, start with four skin care picks that already sell well in the US: medicube Zero Pore Pad 2.0, Anua PDRN Hyaluronic Acid 100 Moisturizing Cream, Biodance Bio-Collagen Real Deep Mask and Round Lab Birch Moisturizing Sunscreen. Together they cover exfoliating, moisturizing, masking and sun care.

Photo: Sgroey via Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 4.0

Olive Young is the first stop for most beauty shoppers in Seoul. Its big stores carry thousands of products, and the shelves change with the season.

So start with numbers. Three of the four picks below were on Amazon’s US Skin Care Best Sellers list on November 9, 2026. The fourth comes from Round Lab, one of the top three brands at Olive Young’s first US store in Pasadena.

That means you can try them at home before your trip, then restock in Seoul. Visitors now make up a third of Olive Young’s in-store sales, up from 2% in 2022.

What visitors buy

  • Over ₩1 trillion. That’s what visitors spent in Olive Young’s Korean stores from January to August 2026, according to the company.
  • Skin care first. It was the most popular category, ahead of makeup and hair care.
  • Derm-style skin care is growing. Visitor sales in that category rose about 78% from a year earlier.
  • Americans shop here too. The US was one of the top five countries by spending.

How to use this list

Treat it as a starter routine for your first visit, not a shopping spree. Each pick covers one step:

  • Exfoliate: medicube Zero Pore Pad 2.0
  • Moisturize: Anua PDRN Hyaluronic Acid 100 Moisturizing Cream
  • Mask: Biodance Bio-Collagen Real Deep Mask, for a slow evening
  • Protect: Round Lab Birch Moisturizing Sunscreen SPF 50, every morning

Already have a favorite for one of these steps? Skip it and use the suitcase space for something new.

In what order

  • Morning: pore pad, cream, sunscreen.
  • Evening: pore pad, cream. On a mask night, the mask goes on last.

Set a budget before you walk in

The average visitor buys 6 items, by Olive Young’s count. About 35% spend ₩100,000 or more in one payment.

Write your list first, one product per routine step. Buy what’s on it, then decide how much is left for extras.

Best Pore Pad: medicube Zero Pore Pad 2.0

medicube
Zero Pore Pad 2.0
Shop on Amazon

Size: 70 pads

Why it's worth it: Each pad has two sides: an embossed side to swipe first and a silky side to finish. The formula pairs lactic acid (AHA) with salicylic acid (BHA), so one pad gives you a quick exfoliating step and skin that looks smoother.

Who it's for: Anyone who wants an easy exfoliating step. If your skin is very sensitive, medicube suggests starting a few times a week.

How to use it: After cleansing, swipe the embossed side from the center of your face outward. Flip and swipe once more with the silky side, then pat in what's left. Keep it away from the eye area.

On Amazon US Skin Care Best Sellers, Nov 9, 2026 (#3 in the category that day).

More to know
  • Key ingredients: 4.5% lactic acid (AHA), 0.45% salicylic acid (BHA), panthenol, allantoin, sodium hyaluronate
  • Where to buy: US (Amazon) and Korea

Best Moisturizer: Anua PDRN Hyaluronic Acid 100 Moisturizing Cream

Anua
PDRN Hyaluronic Acid 100 Moisturizing Cream
Shop on Amazon

Size: 60 ml

Why it's worth it: A lightweight cream in a slim 60 ml tube that fits in a carry-on bag. Anua describes it as fast-absorbing and non-sticky, with a silky finish that doesn't pill under makeup. It pairs PDRN with hyaluronic acid for hydration and a natural-looking glow.

Who it's for: People who dislike heavy creams, or who want a moisturizer that sits well under makeup.

How to use it: Use it morning and night as the last step of your skincare routine. In the morning, sunscreen goes on after it.

On Amazon US Skin Care Best Sellers, Nov 9, 2026 (#15 that day).

More to know
  • Key ingredients: PDRN (from salmon DNA, per Anua), hyaluronic acid
  • Where to buy: US (Amazon) and Korea

How Olive Young works for visitors

  • Help in your language. Stores in busy tourist areas have staff who speak other languages and signs in several languages. Olive Young counts 151 of these stores nationwide.
  • Plan for more than one stop. In an Olive Young survey of visitors in May 2026, shoppers went to 2.8 stores on average. If one store is out of something, the next one may have it.

Tax-free shopping

  • Bring your passport. The physical one: photos and digital copies are often not accepted.
  • Spend ₩15,000 or more in one payment at a participating store to qualify.
  • Ask at the counter. Instant refunds cover up to ₩1,000,000 per receipt and ₩5,000,000 for your whole stay. You must be staying in Korea less than six months.
(Amounts as of Nov. 16, 2026. Source: Trip.com Korea Tax Refund Guide 2026. Rules can change, so check at the counter.)

If you only buy one thing

Make it the sunscreen. It's the one step you use every morning, at 50 ml it fits the US carry-on limit of 3.4 oz (100 ml), and Round Lab was one of the top three brands at Olive Young's US store.

If you already love your sunscreen, pick the pore pad instead. Seventy pads last a while.

Frequently Asked Questions

What should I buy at Olive Young for the first time?

Start with skin care. It is the most popular category with visitors, ahead of makeup and hair care, according to Olive Young. A pore pad, a light moisturizer, a hydrogel mask and a daily sunscreen cover the basics without overfilling your suitcase.

Can I get a tax refund at Olive Young?

Yes, if you spend at least ₩15,000 in one payment at a participating store and show your physical passport. Olive Young tracks visitor purchases through Global Tax Free refund data. Instant refunds at the counter are capped at ₩1,000,000 per receipt and ₩5,000,000 per trip. Amounts as of Nov. 16, 2026, per Trip.com's Korea Tax Refund Guide 2026; rules can change.

Is there an Olive Young in the US?

Yes. Olive Young opened its first US store in Pasadena, California, on May 29, 2026, and runs a US online store at us.oliveyoung.com. The product range is different from the stores in Korea.
