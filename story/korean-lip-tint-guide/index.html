Korean Lip Tint Guide: Finishes, Gradient Lips, Removal

How to choose a Korean lip tint: water, gloss and velvet finishes, how to do a gradient lip, why tints stain, how to take them off, and picks from Seoul.

By The Seoulful Edit Editors / Published 2026-11-18
The short answer

A Korean lip tint is a liquid lip color that leaves a stain, so the color stays after the shine fades. Choose a water or gloss tint for a juicy look, a velvet or blur tint for a soft matte look, and dab it on the center of your lips for a gradient.

Lip tint is one of the easiest Korean makeup buys to try. It is small, light to pack and often costs less than a lipstick.

A tint is not the same as a lipstick or gloss. It leaves a stain, so color stays after the shine wears off. That is why tints suit long days of walking and eating in Seoul.

Three finishes to know

  • Water tint: thin and watery. Daiso sells water tints like the Bonsep Water Veil Tint for ₩3,000.
  • Gloss or glass tint: shiny like a gloss, with a stain underneath. rom&nd describes its Juicy Lasting Tint as a lightweight gloss with a thick, glass-like shine and intense pigment.
  • Velvet or blur tint: soft and matte. Daiso Mall’s lip filters list velvet, blur and matte next to glow and water, so you’ll see these words on labels.

Who should buy what

  • First tint ever: a water tint. It is the easiest to blend and forgiving if you go too far.
  • Glossy, juicy look: a gloss tint like rom&nd’s.
  • Soft, grown-up look: a velvet or blur tint.
  • Only shine, no stain: a gloss, like the ₩5,000 Prelude Dinto La Belle Rose Glass Lip Gloss at Daiso.

The gradient lip

The gradient lip is the Korean look you see most: deeper color in the center, fading toward the edges. rom&nd’s own directions match it. Start from the center of your lips, spread the color outward as much as you like and add another layer for more color.

The trick is to stop before the outer line. Blend the edges with a fingertip so the color looks like it comes from inside the lip.

About staining

Staining is the point of a tint, but it also means color can stay on your lips into the evening. rom&nd says the color of its Juicy Lasting Tint stays true and deepens over time. Remove it with an oil-based cleanser or a lip and eye remover at night.

How to wear a tint, step by step

  • Prep your lips. Tints can look patchy on dry lips. Put on a thin balm, wait a few minutes, then blot it off.
  • Dab the center. One dot on the inner top lip and one on the inner bottom lip.
  • Blend outward. Press your lips together, then tap the edges with a fingertip.
  • Build if you want. Add a second layer in the center for a deeper gradient, or cover your whole lip for a bold look.
  • Add shine last. A clear gloss on top brings back the juicy look once the tint has set.

Before you buy

Swatch on your hand, then wait. rom&nd says the color of its tint deepens over time, so check the swatch after a few minutes.

Read the finish word. On Korean labels, 워터 (water), 글로우 (glow) and 글로스 (gloss) look shiny. 벨벳 (velvet), 블러 (blur) and 매트 (matte) look soft.

Pick by your undertone. Some rom&nd US pages list a color type for each shade, like "Soft Summer." If you know your seasonal color, use it as a shortcut.

Tints and dry lips

  • Watery and gloss tints often feel lighter. rom&nd calls its Juicy Lasting Tint hydrating and comfortable on lips.
  • Velvet and blur tints look softer but can show dry patches. Balm first.
  • Take it off at night. Remove the stain, then put on balm before bed. A ₩1,000 tinted balm from Daiso works for this.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between lip tint and lip gloss?

A tint leaves a stain, and a gloss mostly sits on top. rom&nd says its Juicy Lasting Tint has a glass-like shine that leaves a long-lasting stain, so the color stays after the shine fades. A gloss gives shine, but the color usually wipes off with it.

How do you do a Korean gradient lip?

Put tint only on the inner part of your lips and blend it outward. rom&nd's directions say to start from the center of your lips, spread the color outward as much as you like and add a layer for a more vivid color. Tap the edges with a fingertip so there is no hard line.

How do you remove a lip tint stain?

Use an oil-based cleanser or a lip and eye remover, not just water. Hold a soaked cotton pad on your lips for a few seconds, then wipe gently. Olive Young has a lip and eye remover section (립&아이리무버). A faint stain may stay until the next day, and that is normal.
