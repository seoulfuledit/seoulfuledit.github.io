Korean Sheet Masks: Types, Timing and Where to Buy

Cotton, hydrogel and bio-cellulose sheet masks explained, how long to leave one on, how often to use it, and where to buy masks for less in Seoul.

By The Seoulful Edit Editors / Published 2026-11-24
The short answer

A Korean sheet mask is a single-use sheet soaked in essence. Most boxes say to leave it on 15 to 20 minutes, a 2022 study in Scientific Reports found, and its authors advise staying under 20. Fiber sheets are the most common, hydrogel is a jelly-like gel, and Daiso in Seoul is a low-cost place to buy them.

A sheet mask is the easiest K-beauty souvenir to try. It is one sheet, one pouch, one evening. In Seoul you can pick one up cheaply at Daiso.

The choice is the hard part. Stores sell cotton sheets, jelly-like gels and thin bio-cellulose masks side by side. Here is what each one is, how long to wear it, and where to buy it for less.

What a sheet mask does

A sheet mask holds a watery essence against your face for a few minutes. A Cleveland Clinic dermatologist says sheet masks tend to feel more hydrating than cream masks, because the sheet spreads the moisturizer right where it sits.

She also says the effect is temporary. So think of a mask as a boost before a big day, not a fix.

The three main types

  • Cotton or fiber sheet: the classic. A soft fabric sheet soaked in essence. Some use plant fibers: the Vaseline masks at Daiso use a eucalyptus-fiber sheet, Daiso Mall says. This is the type you will see most, and often the least expensive.
  • Hydrogel: a jelly-like gel sheet instead of fabric. Researchers describe hydrogels as flexible materials made to stick to skin (Polymers, 2025). Some hydrogel masks are meant to stay on for hours, like the one in our first Olive Young list. Always read the box.
  • Bio-cellulose: a sheet made from cellulose that certain bacteria produce, a 2020 paper in the Journal of Cosmetic Dermatology explains. It is a different material from plant cotton, though you use it the same way.

How long to leave it on

  • Most boxes say 15 to 20 minutes. That is what most sheet masks recommend, a 2022 study in Scientific Reports found.
  • Stay under 20 minutes. In that study, exposure to preservatives rose sharply once masks stayed on 25 minutes or longer. The authors think water evaporating from the sheet concentrates what is left in it. They advise under 20 minutes.
  • Some say less. On Daiso Mall, the VT PDRN Glow Sheet Mask says 10 to 20 minutes and the Vaseline masks say about 10.
  • Hydrogel can differ. Follow the box, not a general rule.

How often to use one

No official rule exists. Cleveland Clinic’s dermatologist puts cleansing, daily sunscreen and moisturizer first, and calls a mask a complement to them.

  • Use it when you want a boost, such as the night before a photo day or after a long flight.
  • New to a mask? The American Academy of Dermatology suggests testing a new product on a small patch of skin first. Stop if your skin turns red, itchy or swollen.
  • Sensitive skin: Cleveland Clinic’s dermatologist suggests looking for fragrance-free and dye-free labels.

How to use a sheet mask

  1. Cleanse first. Wash with a gentle cleanser to remove makeup and dirt, Cleveland Clinic advises. Our double cleansing guide covers nights with makeup or sunscreen.
  2. Unfold and line it up. Match the eye, nose and mouth holes, then smooth the sheet out from the center.
  3. Set a timer. Use the time on the box.
  4. Pat in what is left. Daiso’s directions say to peel it off and pat in the leftover essence, with no rinsing.
  5. Finish your routine. Moisturizer goes after the mask. See where it fits in our Korean skincare steps.

Pick a mask by how your skin feels

  • Dry or tight: Cleveland Clinic suggests looking for hyaluronic acid on the label. Our dry skin routine has more.
  • Just want hydration: a cotton or fiber sheet with a watery essence is the simple start.
  • Want a longer, quieter night: try a hydrogel and read its wear time.

Where to buy sheet masks for less in Seoul

  • Daiso: Daiso keeps fixed prices of ₩5,000 or less, BizWatch reported in July 2025. The VT PDRN Glow Sheet Mask and the two Vaseline sheet masks were listed on Daiso Mall (checked Nov. 16, 2026. Source: Daiso Mall). See them all in our Daiso Korea guide.
  • Olive Young Central Myeongdong Town: its "Mask Library" is a large mask section sorted by skin type and concern, Cosinkorea reported in March 2026. Go here for choice, Daiso for price.
  • Buy a few kinds first. Try one cotton and one hydrogel before you buy a whole box of either.

Packing masks for the flight home

  • Carry-on (US flights): TSA allows liquids and gels in containers of 3.4 oz (100 ml) or less, all in one quart-size bag (as of Nov. 16, 2026. Source: TSA. Rules can change, so check before you fly.)
  • Pouch size: the Daiso masks above hold 23 to 25 ml each, under the limit. A stack still fills a quart bag fast.
  • Pack them flat in your checked bag, between clothes, so the pouches do not bend open.

Korean words on the box

  • 마스크팩 (ma-seu-keu-paek): mask pack, the everyday word for a sheet mask.
  • 시트 마스크 (si-teu ma-seu-keu): sheet mask.
  • 하이드로겔 (ha-i-deu-ro-gel): hydrogel.
  • 바이오셀룰로오스 (ba-i-o-sel-lul-lo-o-seu): bio-cellulose.
  • 보습 (bo-seup): moisturizing.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long should you leave a Korean sheet mask on?

Usually 15 to 20 minutes, or whatever your box says. Most sheet masks recommend 15 to 20 minutes, a 2022 study in Scientific Reports found. Its authors advise under 20 minutes, because in their test, exposure to preservatives rose sharply at 25 minutes. Some hydrogel masks are made to stay on longer, so read the label.

How often should you use a sheet mask?

There is no set rule, so follow the box and treat it as an extra. A Cleveland Clinic dermatologist says cleansing, daily sunscreen and moisturizer come first, and a mask complements them. Its effect is temporary, she says, which makes it a good boost before an event. Stop if your skin stings or turns red.

What is the difference between a hydrogel mask and a sheet mask?

A classic sheet mask is fabric, such as cotton or plant fiber, soaked in a watery essence. A hydrogel mask is a jelly-like gel sheet. Researchers describe hydrogels as flexible materials made to stick to skin. Hydrogel masks often have different wear times, so check each box.
