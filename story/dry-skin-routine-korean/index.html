Korean Dry Skin Routine: 3 Steps, Morning and Night

A three-step Korean routine for dry skin: a watery toner, a rich ceramide cream and sunscreen. What to use, how much, in what order, and where to buy it.

By The Seoulful Edit Editors / Published 2026-11-18
The short answer

In the morning, pat on a watery toner, smooth on a rich cream while your skin is still damp, and finish with sunscreen. At night, cleanse gently and repeat the toner and cream without sunscreen, and add more cream whenever your skin feels tight.

Dry skin feels tight after washing and flaky by afternoon. You don’t need ten steps for that. Three products in the morning and two at night cover the basics.

The idea is simple. Put water on first with a thin toner. Then hold it in with a rich cream. In the morning, add sunscreen on top.

Why thin first, then rich

  • Toner adds water. It is watery, so it goes on first and spreads easily over clean skin.
  • Cream keeps it there. The American Academy of Dermatology (AAD) says creams and ointments add more moisture than lotions.
  • Timing matters. The AAD suggests applying moisturizer while your skin is still damp. So go from toner to cream without waiting.
  • Sunscreen goes last. The AAD recommends a broad-spectrum sunscreen with SPF 30 or higher for dry skin, too.

AESTURA, the brand of the cream below, prints the same order in its directions: after toner and serum.

The routine at a glance

  • Morning: VT PDRN Glow Toner, then AESTURA Atobarrier365 Cream, then Round Lab Birch Moisturizing Sunscreen.
  • Night: a gentle cleanser, the toner, then the cream.
  • During the day: more cream on any spot that feels tight. The AAD suggests moisturizing several times a day.

One thing to know first

The AAD suggests fragrance-free products for dry skin. The VT toner lists fragrance, and AESTURA says its cream has less than 1%. If your skin stings, switch to a fragrance-free toner and keep the rest.

The night version

  • Cleanse gently: use a mild cleanser and only enough to remove dirt and oil. The AAD warns against a thick lather on dry skin.
  • Toner, then cream: same two steps as the morning. Put the cream on while your face is still damp.
  • No sunscreen: you only need it for daytime.
  • Humidifier: the AAD suggests running one in your bedroom while you sleep.

Common mistakes with dry skin

  • Long, hot showers: the AAD suggests 5 to 10 minutes in warm water, then patting skin dry.
  • Lotion instead of cream: the AAD says creams and ointments add more moisture than lotions.
  • Waiting too long: apply moisturizer while skin is still damp after washing.
  • Stinging products: the AAD lists alcohol, fragrance and retinoids as things to stop if your skin is dry. If anything burns or stings, stop and ask a dermatologist.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a good Korean skincare routine for dry skin?

Three steps in the morning: a watery toner, a rich cream and sunscreen. At night, cleanse gently and use the toner and cream again. Put the cream on while your skin is still damp, which the AAD recommends for moisturizer.

Should I put toner on before moisturizer?

Yes. Toner goes on first because it is thinner, and cream goes on top. AESTURA's directions for its Atobarrier365 Cream say to apply it after toner and serum. Sunscreen comes last in the morning.

Is cream or lotion better for dry skin?

Cream. The American Academy of Dermatology says ointments and creams add more moisture than lotions. It suggests looking for ingredients like glycerin, hyaluronic acid, dimethicone, petrolatum or shea butter, and choosing fragrance-free products.
