Myeongdong Beauty Shopping Route: A Half-Day Plan

A simple Myeongdong beauty shopping route: the subway stop, Daiso's 12 floors, Olive Young's biggest Myeongdong store, brand shops, and tax refund tips.

By The Seoulful Edit Editors / Published 2026-11-19
The short answer

Take Seoul Subway Line 4 to Myeongdong Station, start at Daiso's multi-floor Myeongdong Station store, then walk into the main streets for Olive Young's large Central Myeongdong Town store, brand shops and pharmacies. Bring your passport for tax refunds, and expect company: Myeongdong drew about 4.5 million foreign visitors in the first half of 2025.

AI-generated model

Myeongdong is where most visitors to Seoul end up, and for good reason. In the first half of 2025, about 4.55 million foreign visitors went to Myeongdong, the most of any area in Korea for the second year running, according to LG U+ roaming data reported by Maeil Business. Korea.net, the government news service, called it the starting point of K-beauty.

The streets are packed with beauty stores, so a plan helps. Here is a simple order that works in half a day.

Getting there

  • Subway: Seoul Subway Line 4, Myeongdong Station. Visit Seoul lists Exits 5 to 10 for the shopping streets.
  • Hours: the area is open daily, but hours vary by store, Visit Seoul says.
  • Map tip: search each store by name in a map app. Many chains have more than one branch here.

What you’ll find

  • Olive Young: eight stores in Myeongdong, Newstop reported in October 2026. The biggest is Central Myeongdong Town, opened in March 2026: three floors, about 1,000 brands and 15,000 products, Cosinkorea reported.
  • Daiso: two Myeongdong stores. The Myeongdong Station store reopened in 2023 in a building of 12 floors, with beauty on the 2nd floor and snacks on the 5th, Businesspost reported.
  • Brand stores: single-brand cosmetics shops line the streets. Visit Seoul lists an Etude House store about 50 m from its Myeongdong map point.
  • Pharmacies: many sell skincare to visitors. Read our pharmacy guide before you buy anything that could be medicine.

A sample route

  1. Daiso Myeongdong Station store. It sits across a main road from the heart of Myeongdong. Many shoppers ride the elevator to the top floor and walk down, Businesspost observed. Stock up on low-cost basics here first.
  2. Cross into the main streets. Head for Olive Young Central Myeongdong Town. Its “Mask Library” holds about 800 sheet masks and other masks, sorted by skin type and function, Cosinkorea reported.
  3. Brand stores and pharmacies. Now you know Olive Young’s prices, so compare before you pay twice for the same thing.
  4. Lunch break. Visit Seoul lists Myeongdong Kyoja, a kal-guksu (knife-cut noodle soup) restaurant, about 50 m from its Myeongdong map point.
  5. One last stop. Go back for anything you skipped, then head home before your bags get too heavy.

Before you go

  • Make a short list. Thousands of products in one store is a lot. Our first Olive Young list helps.
  • Ask for help in English. At Central Myeongdong Town, staff who speak English, Chinese or Japanese wear flag badges, Cosinkorea reported.
  • Save receipts. Keep tax refund receipts together for the airport.
  • Leave room in your bag. Masks and tubes add up fast, and Daiso and Olive Young are only the start of the walk.

Tax-free tips for this route

  • Passport in your pocket, not in the hotel safe. Stores check it at the register.
  • Buy in bigger baskets. A refund needs ₩15,000 or more per purchase, and an immediate refund at the register works for payments under ₩1,000,000 (as of Nov. 16, 2026. Source: VisitKorea).
  • Keep it sealed. Refunded items must stay unopened until you leave Korea (as of Nov. 16, 2026. Source: VisitKorea).

Full steps, airport counters and how much you get back are in our tax refund guide.

Beat the crowds

  • Know the peak. Visit Seoul says Myeongdong fills with Japanese and Chinese tourists during Golden Week, the spring holiday period in both countries.
  • Big stores move faster. Olive Young's Central Myeongdong Town runs 22 staffed checkout counters for large orders, Cosinkorea reported.
  • Use the floor guide at Daiso. The Myeongdong Station store keeps one by the first-floor elevator in Korean, English, Japanese and Chinese, Businesspost reported in 2023.
  • Check hours first. Visit Seoul lists the area as open daily, with hours that vary by store.

Keep walking: Euljiro and Gwangjang Market

Myeongdong does not have to be the end of the day. Korea.net reported in December 2025 that visitors increasingly link Myeongdong with nearby Euljiro and Gwangjang Market into one route.

  • Euljiro: old printing-shop alleys with cafes and bars, popular with younger visitors.
  • Gwangjang Market: a traditional market known for street food.

The roaming data backs it up. Maeil Business reported that areas next to Myeongdong, such as Gwanghui-dong, Pil-dong and Euljiro-dong, gained close to 400,000 foreign visitors each on average in the first half of 2025, compared with a year earlier.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I get to Myeongdong by subway?

Take Seoul Subway Line 4 to Myeongdong Station. Visit Seoul lists Exits 5 to 10 for the shopping streets. From the station, use a map app and search the store name you want, since big stores like Olive Young have several branches in the area.

Is Myeongdong good for Korean skincare shopping?

Yes. Cosmetics are the core of Myeongdong shopping: Maeil Business reported that cosmetics and department stores made up close to 80% of Myeongdong sales in Global Tax Free refund data for early 2025. Olive Young alone runs eight stores in Myeongdong, and foreign shoppers made up 95% of their sales, Newstop reported in October 2026.

What time do Myeongdong shops open?

Hours vary by store. Visit Seoul lists Myeongdong as open daily but does not give one set time for the area. Check each store's hours on a map app before you go, especially if you plan to start early or finish late.
