Daiso Korea Beauty Finds: 4 Picks Under ₩5,000

The sheet masks and toner visitors buy most at Daiso in Seoul, what each one costs, who it suits, and how to pack them for the flight home.

By The Seoulful Edit Editors / Published 2026-11-17
The short answer

At Daiso Korea, start with the VT PDRN Glow Sheet Mask and the two Vaseline sheet masks, ₩1,000 each, which were the top three beauty buys for visitors at Daiso Myeongdong. Add the ₩5,000 VT PDRN Glow Toner, Daiso Mall's best seller in early 2026.

Daiso is Korea’s fixed-price store, and nothing on its shelves costs more than ₩5,000. For many visitors, the beauty aisle is where the basket fills up.

Visitor spending is climbing. Daiso told Hankyung that card spending by foreign visitors rose 60% in 2025, then another 70% in January to March 2026. At the Myeongdong store, sheet masks led the beauty sales.

What’s on this list

  • Top sheet mask: VT PDRN Glow Sheet Mask, ₩1,000
  • Top toner: VT PDRN Glow Toner, ₩5,000
  • Richer mask: Vaseline Sheet Mask, Moisturizing, ₩1,000
  • Lighter mask: Vaseline Sheet Mask, Hydrating, ₩1,000

All four come to ₩8,000. Prices are from Daiso Mall, checked in November 2026.

Best Sheet Mask: VT PDRN Glow Sheet Mask

VT
PDRN Glow Sheet Mask
Not sold in the US. Where to buy: Daiso stores in Korea, ₩1,000

Size: 1 sheet, 25 g

Why it's worth it: A thick sheet that turns plump and jelly-like as it soaks up a watery essence. At ₩1,000 you can buy one for every night of your trip.

Who it's for: Anyone who wants an easy, low-cost hydrating mask. Daiso lists it for all skin types.

How to use it: After cleansing, leave it on for 10 to 20 minutes. Peel it off and pat in the leftover essence. No rinsing.

#1 beauty buy for visitors at Daiso Myeongdong (Daiso data reported by Hankyung, Apr. 2026).

More to know
  • Key ingredients: Niacinamide, glycerin, panthenol, sodium hyaluronate, sodium DNA from wild ginseng extract (what VT calls PDRN). Contains fragrance.
  • Where to buy: Daiso stores in Korea, ₩1,000

Best Toner: VT PDRN Glow Toner

VT
PDRN Glow Toner
Not sold in the US. Where to buy: Daiso stores in Korea, ₩5,000

Size: 200 ml (6.8 fl. oz.)

Why it's worth it: A 200 ml bottle of milky, watery toner for ₩5,000, the top price at Daiso. It has the same PDRN ingredient as the mask, so the two pair easily.

Who it's for: Anyone who goes through toner fast and wants a big bottle for little money. Listed for all skin types.

How to use it: After cleansing, put some on a cotton pad or your palms and smooth it from the center of your face outward. VT also suggests pouring it into a mist bottle.

Best seller on Daiso Mall, Jan. 1 to June 21, 2026 (Daiso data reported by Herald Business, July 2026).

More to know
  • Key ingredients: Niacinamide, glycerin, sodium hyaluronate, sodium DNA from wild ginseng extract. Contains fragrance.
  • Where to buy: Daiso stores in Korea, ₩5,000

Best Value Mask: Vaseline Sheet Mask, Moisturizing

Vaseline
Sheet Mask, Moisturizing
Not sold in the US. Where to buy: Daiso stores in Korea, ₩1,000

Size: 1 sheet, 23 ml (0.78 fl. oz.)

Why it's worth it: A Daiso Korea sheet mask from a name every American knows. The essence is cream-type, with ceramide NP and a little petrolatum, so it feels richer than the hydrating one.

Who it's for: Skin that feels dry or tight and wants a creamier mask.

How to use it: On clean skin, smooth it over your eyes, nose and mouth and leave it on about 10 minutes. Remove it and pat in what's left.

#2 beauty buy for visitors at Daiso Myeongdong (Daiso data reported by Hankyung, Apr. 2026).

More to know
  • Key ingredients: Ceramide NP, petrolatum, glycerin, cholesterol. Contains limonene and grapefruit peel oil.
  • Where to buy: Daiso stores in Korea, ₩1,000

Best for Hydration: Vaseline Sheet Mask, Hydrating

Vaseline
Sheet Mask, Hydrating
Not sold in the US. Where to buy: Daiso stores in Korea, ₩1,000

Size: 1 sheet, 23 ml (0.78 fl. oz.)

Why it's worth it: The lighter of the two Vaseline masks. Its ampoule-type essence carries hyaluronic acid, on the same eucalyptus-fiber sheet.

Who it's for: Anyone who wants water more than richness, or a lighter mask for a warm day.

How to use it: Same as the moisturizing one: about 10 minutes on clean skin, then pat in the rest.

#3 beauty buy for visitors at Daiso Myeongdong (Daiso data reported by Hankyung, Apr. 2026).

More to know
  • Key ingredients: Sodium hyaluronate, hyaluronic acid, glycerin, betaine, petrolatum. Contains limonene and grapefruit peel oil.
  • Where to buy: Daiso stores in Korea, ₩1,000

How to shop Daiso in Seoul

Prices are fixed. Nothing at Daiso costs more than ₩5,000, and there are about 1,500 stores in Korea, so one is rarely far away.

  • Check stock first: the Daiso Mall app shows which stores have an item and offers store pickup.
  • Myeongdong: Hankyung calls it the Daiso store where visitor buying shows most clearly, and masks lead its beauty sales.
  • Buy when you see it: the VT toner sold out widely after launch (Herald Business), so don't save it for your last day.

Tax-free at Daiso

Bring your physical passport. Tax-free shopping starts at ₩15,000 in one payment. (Amounts as of Nov. 16, 2026. Source: Trip.com Korea Tax Refund Guide 2026. Rules can change, so check at the counter.) Daiso is among the stores with a Tax Free logo, where the tax can come off right at the register.

All four picks here add up to ₩8,000. To reach ₩15,000, put everything in one basket and pay once.

Packing for the flight home

  • Carry-on (US flights): liquids and gels must be 3.4 oz (100 ml) or less per container, all in one quart-size bag.
  • Toner: the 200 ml bottle is over the limit. It goes in your checked bag.
  • Masks: each pouch holds 23 to 25 ml, under the limit, but a stack fills your quart bag fast. Lay them flat in your checked bag instead.

Frequently Asked Questions

What should I buy at Daiso in Korea for skincare?

Start with sheet masks. The VT PDRN Glow Sheet Mask and two Vaseline sheet masks were the top three beauty buys for visitors at Daiso Myeongdong (Hankyung, April 2026). Each costs ₩1,000. For a toner, the ₩5,000 VT PDRN Glow Toner was Daiso Mall's best seller in the first half of 2026.

How much is skincare at Daiso Korea?

₩5,000 or less. Daiso keeps fixed prices that top out at ₩5,000. On Daiso Mall in November 2026, the sheet masks here were ₩1,000 each and the 200 ml VT toner was ₩5,000.

Can I bring Daiso sheet masks and toner in my carry-on?

The masks, yes. The 200 ml toner, no. TSA allows liquids in containers of 3.4 oz (100 ml) or less, all in one quart-size bag. Each mask pouch holds 23 to 25 ml, but a stack is easier to pack flat in your checked bag.
