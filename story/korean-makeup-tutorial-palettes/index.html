Korean Makeup Tutorial: A Soft Pink Look in 7 Steps

A step-by-step Korean makeup tutorial for a soft pink, dewy look: where highlight, contour and blush go, plus palettes from Olive Young's ranking.

By The Seoulful Edit Editors / Published 2026-11-23
The short answer

Start with a thin, dewy cushion base. Shade lightly under the cheekbones and along the jaw, then sweep a sheer pink blush high on the cheeks, just under the eyes. Add a balm highlight to the cheekbones, nose bridge and chin, a soft pink palette on the lids, and a glossy gradient lip.

AI-generated model

Korean makeup is less about covering the skin and more about making it look fresh. Popular Korean makeup pins on Pinterest tend to share the same parts: dewy skin, a soft pink flush high on the cheeks, a glow on the high points of the face and glossy lips.

This tutorial breaks that look into seven steps, with one product for each. Every pick comes from Olive Young Korea’s makeup sales ranking, checked on Nov. 22, 2026, and six of the seven are sold on Amazon US through the brand’s own store.

The look in one line

Thin dewy base, light shading, sheer blush right under the eyes, balm glow, soft pink eyes and a gradient gloss.

Before you start

  • Skin first. A dewy base sits better on moisturized skin. Our Korean cushion foundation guide covers prep and shade matching.
  • Go light, then build. Every step here works in thin layers. It is easier to add than to take away.
  • Match your undertone. Cool, pink-toned shades suit cool undertones. Peachy, warm shades suit warm undertones. Both versions of rom&nd’s contour and CLIO’s undertone shade guide help here.

The seven steps

  1. Base: a thin layer of cushion, tapped in.
  2. Contour: light shading under the cheekbones, along the jaw and at the sides of the nose.
  3. Blush: sheer pink, high on the cheeks, just under the eyes.
  4. Highlight: balm on the cheekbones, forehead center, nose bridge and chin.
  5. Eyes: a soft pink palette, with shimmer on the lid center and the aegyo-sal.
  6. Brows: soft and straight. See our Korean brow guide.
  7. Lips: a glossy gradient. Our Korean lip tint guide shows how to blur the edges.

The products for each step, with how to use them, are below.

The face map: where each step goes

  • Highlight: center of the forehead, bridge of the nose, tops of the cheekbones, cupid's bow and the center of the chin.
  • Contour: under the cheekbones, along the jaw, the sides of the nose and the hairline at the temples. Keep it light.
  • Blush: high on the cheeks, starting right under the eyes and spreading out toward the cheekbones. Soft edges, no circles.
  • Lips: deeper color at the center, blended out to the edges.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Korean makeup look?

It focuses on skin that looks fresh and dewy rather than heavily covered. A thin cushion base, light shading, sheer blush high on the cheeks, a glow on the high points of the face and a glossy gradient lip are the usual parts.

Where do you put blush for Korean makeup?

High on the cheeks, starting just under the eyes and blending out toward the cheekbones. Keep the edges soft so it looks like a natural flush rather than a circle.

What is aegyo-sal in makeup?

Aegyo-sal is the small puff of skin just under the lower lash line. In Korean makeup, a little shimmer or a light shade on that area makes the eyes look brighter and softer.
