Gyeongju Day Trip: King Munmu's Sea Tomb and Silla Sites

A Gyeongju day trip from Seoul by KTX, built around King Munmu of the new drama Munmu: his tomb in the sea, Gameunsa and Wolji Pond at night.

By The Seoulful Edit Editors / Published 2026-11-22
The short answer

Take the KTX from Seoul Station to Gyeongju Station, about 2 hours 10 minutes by Gyeongju City's count. Spend the day on the east coast, where King Munmu's tomb sits in the sea off Bonggil Beach, see the twin pagodas at Gameunsa Temple Site, then return to town for Donggung Palace and Wolji Pond lit up at night.

Photo: Korea Tourism Organization

Gyeongju was the capital of the Silla kingdom. Today it is a small city of royal tombs, temple sites and palace ponds, about two hours from Seoul by train.

More foreign visitors are making the trip. Gyeongju City counted 707,197 foreign visitors from January to June 2026, up 19.0% from a year earlier, based on Korea Tourism Data Lab figures (reported Aug. 20, 2026).

There is a new reason to go, too. Munmu, a KBS2 historical drama about the wars of Korea’s Three Kingdoms, premiered on Nov. 14, 2026, and is streaming on Netflix. Its title king, Munmu, ruled Silla from 661 to 681. This guide follows the places tied to him, from his tomb in the sea to the palace pond dug in his reign.

Who was King Munmu?

  • The king: Munmu ruled Silla from 661 to 681, the Korea Heritage Service says.
  • His last wish: the Samguk Sagi, an early history of Korea’s Three Kingdoms, records that he asked to be cremated and buried in the East Sea. The Korea Heritage Service says he wanted to become a dragon and guard the kingdom from Japanese raiders. Treat the dragon part as legend.
  • His son: King Sinmun finished the temple his father started and built a pavilion facing the sea tomb. Both are on this route.

Getting there from Seoul

  • KTX train: high-speed KTX trains run from Seoul Station to Gyeongju Station. Gyeongju City puts the trip at about 2 hours 10 minutes. Times vary by train, so check the Korail timetable.
  • The station name: Gyeongju Station is the former Singyeongju Station, the KTX stop outside downtown. Older maps may still show the old name.
  • From the station to town: buses 700 and 710 run from Gyeongju Station to the Donggung Palace and Wolji Pond stop in about 25 minutes, the Gyeongju National Museum says.
  • To the coast: the King Munmu sites are on the east coast. Gyeongju City lists buses 150, 150-1 and 1150 from the Express Bus Terminal downtown to the Daejonggyo stop, then about a 10-minute walk to the marine museum. A taxi saves time if you are short on hours.

The King Munmu route

  1. Munmu Daewang Marine History Museum. Start here to get the story before you see the sea. Gyeongju City lists hours of 10:00 to 18:00 from March to October and 10:00 to 17:00 from November to February. It is closed on Mondays, Jan. 1, and on Seollal and Chuseok days. Adult tickets are ₩3,000, but entry is free until Dec. 31, 2026 (as of Nov. 22, 2026. Source: Gyeongju City).
  2. Igyeondae Pavilion. A three-minute walk from the museum, Gyeongju City says. King Sinmun built a pavilion here, at the spot with the best view of his father’s sea tomb. The building you see today was rebuilt on the excavated site. Legend says Sinmun received a flute called Manpasikjeok here, from a dragon. The story comes from the Samguk Yusa, a later collection of history and legend.
  3. Tomb of King Munmu (Daewangam). A rock formation about 200 meters off the shore at Bonggil Beach, about five minutes by car from the museum. The Korea Heritage Service lists it as the king’s underwater tomb, designated a Historic Site in 1967. Stand on the beach and look out at the rocks.
  4. Gameunsa Temple Site. King Munmu began this temple to guard against Japanese raiders, and King Sinmun completed it in 682, the Korea Heritage Service says. The name means a temple of gratitude. Only the stone foundations and two three-story stone pagodas remain, and the pagodas are a National Treasure. Legend says a channel under the main hall let the dragon king come in from the sea.
  5. Back to town: Gyeongju National Museum. If you arrive before 18:00, stop in. The permanent galleries are free, the museum says. It holds fragments of King Munmu’s stone monument, which records his cremation and sea burial.
  6. Donggung Palace and Wolji Pond at night. The Samguk Sagi says the pond was dug in 674, during Munmu’s reign. Three buildings were restored in 1980, and at night their lights reflect in the water. VisitKorea lists hours of 09:00 to 22:00, with last entry at 21:30, open every day. Adult tickets are ₩3,000 (as of Nov. 22, 2026. Source: VisitKorea).

Day trip or overnight?

  • Day trip: take an early KTX, do the coast in the morning and early afternoon, and finish at Wolji Pond. Check the last KTX back to Seoul before you go.
  • One night: stay downtown, see Wolji Pond after dark without watching the clock, and give the national museum a full morning the next day.

Small tips

  • Wear walking shoes. The temple site and the pond paths are mostly gravel and dirt.
  • Go to Wolji Pond after sunset. The buildings are lit at night, and the pond is open until 22:00.
  • Carry some cash. Small shops near the coast may prefer it.

Before you go

  • Book the train early. KTX seats from Seoul fill up on weekends. Check times and book on the Korail website or app.
  • Plan the coast first. The tomb, Igyeondae and Gameunsa are on the east coast, far from downtown. Go there in the daylight and save Wolji Pond for the evening.
  • Check closing days. The Munmu Daewang Marine History Museum closes on Mondays. The Gyeongju National Museum has a regular closing day on the second Monday of March, June, September and December.
  • Use a Korean map app. Naver Map or KakaoMap shows bus numbers and stops better than Google Maps in Korea.

About the drama Munmu

Munmu is a 28-episode KBS2 historical drama about the leaders of Korea's Three Kingdoms at war. It premiered on Nov. 14, 2026, and is also on Netflix. KBS said it reached No. 1 on Netflix's Top 10 TV list in South Korea on Nov. 17, 2026 (Source: Sports Kyunghyang). This guide is not affiliated with KBS or Netflix, and we use no images from the drama.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I get from Seoul to Gyeongju?

Take the KTX from Seoul Station to Gyeongju Station. Gyeongju City puts the trip at about 2 hours 10 minutes, but times vary by train, so check the Korail timetable. Gyeongju Station is outside downtown. Buses 700 and 710 run from the station toward Donggung Palace and the national museum in about 25 minutes.

Can you visit King Munmu's underwater tomb?

You see it from the shore. The Tomb of King Munmu is a rock formation about 200 meters off the coast near Bonggil Beach. The Korea Heritage Service describes it as the king's underwater tomb, so plan to view it from the beach rather than go out to the rocks.

Is one day enough for Gyeongju?

One day covers this King Munmu route if you start early and focus on the coast and Wolji Pond. Gyeongju has many more Silla sites, so stay a night if you also want the national museum, the royal tomb park downtown and a slower evening.
