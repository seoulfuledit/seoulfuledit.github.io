Aestura vs Illiyoon: Which Korean Moisturizer to Buy

Aestura Atobarrier365 or Illiyoon Ceramide Ato? Texture, key ingredients, sizes and where to buy each, so you can pick the right cream for your skin.

By The Seoulful Edit Editors / Published 2026-11-19
The short answer

Pick Aestura Atobarrier365 Cream if you want a face cream in a small 80 ml tube that is easy to reorder on Amazon US. Pick Illiyoon Ceramide Ato Concentrate Cream if you want a bigger fragrance-free, vegan cream that Amorepacific sells as body care.

Both creams come from Amorepacific, and both sit on the dry-and-sensitive shelf at Olive Young. They look alike on paper. In practice, they suit different jobs.

Aestura’s cream is a face cream in a small tube. Illiyoon’s comes in bigger tubes, and Amorepacific files it under body care. That one difference explains most of what follows.

The short version

  • Texture: the brand calls Aestura soft and non-sticky. It calls Illiyoon melting, with a smooth finish.
  • Key ingredient: Aestura lists ceramide NP, cholesterol and niacinamide. Illiyoon leads with a ceramide it calls CPP-40, plus madecassoside.
  • Size: Aestura comes in 80 ml. Illiyoon comes in 150 ml to 230 ml in Korea, and 6.76 fl. oz. on Amazon US.
  • Fragrance: both are fragrance-free, according to the brand pages. Illiyoon’s label also says vegan.
  • Where to buy: Olive Young in Seoul carries both. On Amazon US, each is sold by the brand’s own seller.

Pick Aestura if…

  • You want a cream for your face only.
  • You need a small tube that fits a carry-on bag.
  • You like niacinamide in a moisturizer.

Pick Illiyoon if…

  • You want one cream for face, arms and legs.
  • You share skincare with a partner or family.
  • You prefer a vegan, fragrance-free label.

What the labels actually say

Aestura’s tube reads “Moisturizing & strengthening skin barrier” and “For dry & sensitive skin.” Illiyoon’s reads “For Sensitive & Dry Skin,” “Fragrance Free,” “9 FREE” and “Vegan.”

Those are the brands’ words. Both are cosmetics, not treatments. If you have a skin condition, ask a dermatologist what to use.

Ingredients they share

Read the full lists and the two creams overlap more than you’d expect. Both contain ceramide NP, cholesterol, phytosphingosine, squalane, glycerin and mannitol.

The differences are in the extras. Aestura adds niacinamide and allantoin. Illiyoon adds madecassoside, black soybean oil, ginseng water and a few plant extracts.

If you react to plant extracts, note that Aestura’s list has none. If you avoid animal-derived ingredients, Illiyoon is the one labeled vegan.

Can you use both?

Yes. Following each brand’s directions, that means Aestura on your face morning and evening, and Illiyoon on your arms, legs and any dry spots on the body.

Reading the box in the store

Korean packaging mixes Korean and English. Three words help on the shelf:

  • 민감 (min-gam): sensitive
  • 건조 (geon-jo): dry
  • 무향 (mu-hyang): fragrance-free

Illiyoon’s front label prints 민감&건조 피부용, “for sensitive & dry skin,” next to the English.

How to use a barrier cream in your routine

  • Last step at night: cleanser, then toner or serum if you use them, then the cream.
  • In the morning: cream first, then sunscreen on top.
  • On the body: Illiyoon suggests its Ceramide Ato Lotion first and the cream on spots that stay dry.
  • Amount: cover the area in a thin, even layer. Add more on dry patches rather than over the whole face.

Buying in Seoul vs the US

In Seoul: Olive Young carries both creams, sometimes as sets with minis. On November 16, 2026, Amorepacific's own mall listed Aestura's 80 ml at ₩33,000 and Illiyoon's 230 ml at ₩21,900 before discounts.

In the US: buy from the brand's own Amazon seller. Check that the listing says "Sold by AESTURA Official" or "Sold by ILLIYOON US." If a different seller name shows up, look again before you buy.

Sizes differ by market. Illiyoon comes in 150 ml at Olive Young, 230 ml on Amorepacific's mall and 6.76 fl. oz. on Amazon US. Compare the price per ml, not the price per tub.

Patch test first

The American Academy of Dermatology suggests testing a new product before you add it to your routine. Apply it to a quarter-sized spot, such as the inside of your elbow, twice a day for 7 to 10 days.

If your skin reacts, stop using it. If irritation keeps coming back, a dermatologist can do a patch test to find the cause.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Aestura or Illiyoon better for dry skin?

Both are made for dry, sensitive skin, so choose by where you use it. Aestura Atobarrier365 Cream is a face cream in an 80 ml tube. Illiyoon Ceramide Ato Concentrate Cream is listed under body care by Amorepacific and comes in larger tubes.

Are Aestura and Illiyoon made by the same company?

Yes. Both brands belong to Amorepacific, and both creams are sold on Amorepacific's own online mall. Aestura's cream is a face cream. Illiyoon's is filed under body care and sold in larger sizes.

Can I buy Aestura and Illiyoon in the US?

Yes. As of November 16, 2026, Aestura Atobarrier365 Cream is sold on Amazon US by AESTURA Official, and Illiyoon Ceramide Ato Concentrate Cream by ILLIYOON US. In Seoul, Olive Young carries both.
