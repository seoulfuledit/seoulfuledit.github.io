Seongsu Seoul Beauty Shopping: A Simple Half-Day Route

Seongsu, Seoul for beauty shoppers: the subway exits, Olive Young N Seongsu, AMORE Seongsu, pop-up streets and a half-day route you can save.

By The Seoulful Edit Editors / Published 2026-11-25
The short answer

Take Seoul Subway Line 2 to Seongsu Station. Olive Young N Seongsu is about 118 m from Exit 4, and AMORE Seongsu is about 264 m from Exit 2, per Visit Seoul. Then walk Yeonmujang-gil for pop-ups. Skip Mondays if you want AMORE Seongsu, which closes that day.

AI-generated model

Seongsu-dong used to be a district of shoe factories, printing shops and auto parts suppliers, Visit Seoul says. Today the old warehouses hold cafes, flagship stores and pop-ups, and beauty is a big part of the mix. Olive Young opened Olive Young N Seongsu here in late November 2024, and CJ Newsroom called it the chain’s largest store in Korea in January 2025.

Seongsu is not where you go to fill a suitcase fast. It is where you test, compare and get your skin checked. Here is how to plan half a day.

Getting there

  • Subway: Seoul Subway Line 2, Seongsu Station. Visit Seoul lists the main stops below about 120 to 540 m from Exits 2, 3 and 4.
  • Other way in: Seoul Forest Station on the Suin-Bundang Line, listed by Visit Seoul next to D Museum and Seoul Forest park.
  • Map tip: search each store by its English name in a map app. Seongsu has several Olive Young locations, and their names sound alike.
  • Avoid Mondays if AMORE Seongsu is on your list. It is closed that day, Visit Seoul says.

What you’ll find

  • Olive Young N Seongsu: five floors and about 4,620 square meters, nearly nine times the size of an average Olive Young Town store, CJ Newsroom reported. The first floor has a pop-up zone and cafe. Floors 2 and 3 hold 12 zones such as Color Makeup and Daily Skincare. The 4th floor is for VIP members. Staff speak several languages, and shelves carry electronic English labels. Visit Seoul lists it at 13 Yeonmujang 7-gil, open daily 10:00 to 22:00, about 118 m from Exit 4.
  • AMORE Seongsu: Amorepacific’s flagship, with Sulwhasoo, Laneige, HERA, Innisfree, ETUDE, IOPE and Mamonde under one roof. You can have foundation and lipstick matched to your skin tone, and reservations are recommended for that. Visit Seoul lists it at 7 Achasan-ro 11-gil, open Tuesday to Sunday 10:30 to 20:30, about 264 m from Exit 2.
  • Olive Young Beauty Mansion Seongsu: four floors and about 1,653 square meters, opened June 30, 2026, Olive Young said. Floors 2 and 3 focus on makeup and skincare services, including 3D skin diagnostics and a Beauty Device Studio for trying K-beauty devices. Olive Young placed it to link Yeonmujang-gil with the northern part of Seongsu.
  • OLIVE BETTER Seongsu: Olive Young’s wellness store, opened Oct. 30, 2026, on four floors. It has a gua sha island, inner beauty displays and a Better Bar serving shakes and teas, Olive Young said.
  • Postes Seongsu Boutique: a fragrance shop at 77 Yeonmujang-gil, open daily 11:00 to 20:00, about 397 m from Exit 3, Visit Seoul says. You can write a note at its mailbox, and Postes mails it for free.
  • Yeonmujang-gil: the main street for murals, shopfronts and seasonal pop-ups. Visit Seoul lists it as open 24 hours.

A sample route

  1. AMORE Seongsu (from Exit 2). Go first, when it opens at 10:30, if you booked a custom shade.
  2. Olive Young N Seongsu (near Exit 4). Check the first-floor pop-up, then shop floors 2 and 3. Our first Olive Young list keeps the basket short.
  3. Yeonmujang-gil. Walk slowly. Visit Seoul notes that walls and storefronts change with brand collaborations, so look down side streets.
  4. Postes Seongsu Boutique. A quiet stop for fragrance after the crowds.
  5. Beauty Mansion or OLIVE BETTER. Pick one, based on whether you want skin services or wellness snacks. Search the name in your map app for the route.
  6. Seoul Forest. End at the park if you have time, then take the Suin-Bundang Line from Seoul Forest Station.

Before you go

  • Book services early. Olive Young N Seongsu releases lesson slots every Friday at noon for the following week, Visit Seoul says.
  • Wear walking shoes. Stops are spread out, and the side streets are worth a look.
  • Save receipts together for tax refunds at the airport.
  • Check hours on the day. Pop-ups and store hours change often in Seongsu.

Pop-ups: how to find this week's

  • Expect change. Visit Seoul describes Seongsu as a place where a new concept store replaces yesterday's pop-up. Any list goes out of date fast.
  • Check the big stores first. Olive Young N Seongsu has a pop-up zone on its first floor, and Beauty Mansion has pop-up and shop-in-shop spaces on its first floor, Olive Young says.
  • Search the night before. Brands announce pop-ups on their own Instagram accounts. Search "Seongsu pop-up" with the date, then save the address in your map app.

Tax refunds in Seongsu

  • Bring your passport. Stores check it at the register.
  • Know the minimum. A refund needs ₩15,000 or more per purchase, and an immediate refund at the register works for single payments under ₩1,000,000, up to ₩5,000,000 for the trip (as of Nov. 17, 2026. Source: VisitKorea. Rules can change, so check at the counter).
  • Keep it sealed. Refunded items must stay new and unopened until you leave Korea (as of Nov. 17, 2026. Source: VisitKorea).

Airport steps and how much you get back are in our tax refund guide.

Seongsu or Myeongdong?

  • Seongsu suits trying things: skin and scalp scans, makeup lessons, custom shades and pop-ups.
  • Myeongdong suits stocking up: many Olive Young branches, Daiso and brand shops on a few streets. See our Myeongdong route.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I get to Seongsu-dong in Seoul?

Take Seoul Subway Line 2 to Seongsu Station. Visit Seoul lists Olive Young N Seongsu about 118 m from Exit 4, AMORE Seongsu about 264 m from Exit 2 and Yeonmujang-gil about 533 m from Exit 3. Seoul Forest Station on the Suin-Bundang Line is another way in, near Seoul Forest park.

Is Seongsu good for K-beauty shopping?

Yes, especially for trying products. Olive Young N Seongsu has about 4,620 square meters over five floors, and CJ Newsroom called it the chain's largest store in Korea in January 2025. AMORE Seongsu offers custom foundation and lipstick, and Olive Young opened Beauty Mansion Seongsu on June 30, 2026, with skincare and makeup services.

Do I need a reservation at Olive Young N Seongsu?

Not to shop. Visit Seoul says some services need booking ahead, such as the Home Care Lesson and Pick Your Vibe, with new slots opening every Friday at noon for the following week on the Olive Young N website. Makeup sessions and personal color consultations also take advance reservations.
