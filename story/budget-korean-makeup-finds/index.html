Budget Korean Makeup: Daiso and Olive Young Finds

Korean makeup from ₩1,000 at Daiso and under ₩15,000 at Olive Young: lip balm, tint, cushion, brows and liner, with prices from official store pages.

By The Seoulful Edit Editors / Published 2026-11-20
The short answer

For budget Korean makeup, start at Daiso, where every makeup item we saw on Daiso Mall's makeup page cost ₩1,000 to ₩5,000. Then add one or two Olive Young best sellers under ₩15,000, like the rom&nd Juicy Lasting Tint or the Peripera Speedy Skinny Brow.

You don’t need a big budget to bring Korean makeup home. Every makeup item we saw on Daiso Mall’s makeup page cost ₩1,000 to ₩5,000 on Nov. 16, 2026.

Olive Young costs more, but some of its top makeup sellers list at about ₩6,000. Here is what to look for in each store, with the prices we saw.

Who should buy what

  • First-timers: a ₩1,000 lip balm and a ₩3,000 tint from Daiso. Low risk if the shade is wrong.
  • Base makeup on a budget: Daiso’s ₩5,000 cushions.
  • Brow and liner fans: Olive Young’s brow pencils and liners, which rank high in its makeup sales.
  • One bigger buy: a tint from Olive Young’s ranking, such as rom&nd’s.

Daiso: makeup for ₩1,000 to ₩5,000

  • Orix Color Lip Balm, ₩1,000. A 4.8 g tinted balm in shades like Peach Coral and Strawberry Pink. Daiso Mall showed 933 reviews and 6,000+ purchases for Peach Coral.
  • Stylish Auto Lip Liner Pencil, ₩1,000. A twist-up liner to shape your lips before tint.
  • Bonsep Water Veil Tint, ₩3,000. A watery lip tint. Our lip tint guide explains how to wear it.
  • Prelude Dinto La Belle Rose Liquid Cheek, ₩3,000. A 10 ml liquid blush with 6,200+ purchases.
  • Clavue Balancing Cooling Primer, ₩3,000. The highest purchase count among the items we saw: 10,000+ bought and 1,186 reviews.
  • Tag Dewy Skin Cushion, ₩5,000. A 15 g cushion compact with 6,400+ purchases. New to cushions? Read our beginner’s guide first.
  • Bonsep Slim Fixing Browcara, ₩3,000. A tinted brow mascara.
  • Twoedit Banjil Coating Makeup Fixer, ₩5,000. A 100 ml setting spray.

Olive Young: best sellers under ₩15,000

These were in Olive Young Korea’s makeup sales ranking on Nov. 16, 2026. Prices are list prices, and many were on sale for less.

  • Holika Holika My Fave Piece Eyeshadow, from ₩6,000 (#6). Single shadows, so you buy only the colors you want.
  • Peripera Speedy Skinny Brow, ₩6,000 (#9). A slim brow pencil, also sold in a set with brow mascara.
  • Clio Sharp So Simple Waterproof Pencil Liner, ₩11,000 (#20). A waterproof pencil eyeliner.
  • rom&nd The Juicy Lasting Tint, ₩13,000 (#19). A glossy lip tint. rom&nd’s US store sells its Juicy Lasting Tint for $14, so you can reorder at home.

Before you pay

  • Swatch first. Lip shades can look different on your skin than in the tube.
  • Start small. Buy one shade, wear it for a day, and go back for more. Daiso and Olive Young stores are everywhere in Seoul.
  • Check the size. Low prices sometimes mean small sizes, like the 4.8 g lip balm. Read the grams or milliliters on the tag.

How to shop makeup at Daiso

  • Know the brands. Daiso's makeup shelf carries Korean brands such as Bonsep, Tag, Clavue, Merzy, Holika Holika and Prelude Dinto.
  • Use the store finder. Daiso Mall has a "find products in store" tool (매장 상품 찾기). Not every branch has every shade.
  • Read the purchase badge. Daiso Mall shows badges like "구매 6천+" (6,000+ bought). It shows what other shoppers pick.
  • Read the shade number. Daiso puts the shade in brackets before the name, like [04 레드워터] (04 Red Water). Screenshot it so you grab the same one in the store.

How to shop makeup at Olive Young

Two prices on the tag. Olive Young often shows a list price and a lower sale price. Many November items were marked "올영픽" (Olive Young Pick) deals when we checked.

Check the ranking on your phone. The 랭킹 (ranking) page on Olive Young's Korean site has a makeup tab. Screenshot it and show staff the item.

Use the testers. For cushions and tints, try two shades side by side on your jaw or hand before you pay.

A starter kit for under ₩20,000

  • Lips: Orix Color Lip Balm, ₩1,000, and Bonsep Water Veil Tint, ₩3,000 (Daiso).
  • Base: Tag Dewy Skin Cushion, ₩5,000 (Daiso).
  • Cheeks: Prelude Dinto La Belle Rose Liquid Cheek, ₩3,000 (Daiso).
  • Brows: Peripera Speedy Skinny Brow, ₩6,000 list price (Olive Young).

That adds up to ₩18,000 at the prices we saw on Nov. 16, 2026.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Daiso makeup good in Korea?

It is a low-risk way to try new colors and formats. Daiso Korea stocks makeup from brands like Bonsep, Tag, Clavue and Prelude Dinto, and every makeup item we saw on Daiso Mall cost ₩1,000 to ₩5,000 (Nov. 16, 2026). Swatch shades on your hand first, since a used lip product can't go back.

What is the cheapest makeup at Olive Young?

Brow and eye products are often the lowest priced. In Olive Young Korea's makeup sales ranking on Nov. 16, 2026, the Holika Holika My Fave Piece Eyeshadow started at ₩6,000 and the Peripera Speedy Skinny Brow listed at ₩6,000. Many items were also on sale, so check the shelf tag.

Is makeup cheaper at Daiso or Olive Young?

Daiso is cheaper per item. Its makeup topped out at ₩5,000 on Daiso Mall, while most of Olive Young's top makeup sellers listed at ₩10,000 to ₩40,000 before sales (Nov. 16, 2026). Olive Young has more brands, shades and testers, so many shoppers get basics at Daiso and one or two favorites at Olive Young.
