Korean Body Care Picks: Cream, Wash and Scrub

Korean body care worth a spot in your bag: a fragrance-free ceramide cream, a head-to-toe wash, a family-size lotion, the Italy towel, and how to pack them.

By The Seoulful Edit Editors / Published 2026-11-22
The short answer

For Korean body care, start with a fragrance-free ceramide cream like ILLIYOON's Ceramide Ato Concentrate Cream for dry spots, plus a gentle wash and an Italy towel for weekly scrubbing. Apply cream to damp skin after a short warm shower, and pack anything over 100 mL in checked luggage.

Korean body care is less about fancy scents and more about big, plain basics. Shelves in Seoul are full of family-size lotions and pump washes, and the green Italy towel hangs nearby.

That makes a simple routine easy to build: wash gently, scrub once in a while, and seal in moisture with a thick cream. Here is what to pick and how to use it.

Who needs what

  • Dry, tight skin: a fragrance-free cream, not a thin lotion. The American Academy of Dermatology (AAD) says creams and ointments work better for dry skin.
  • Easily irritated skin: fewer products, no added fragrance, and patch-test anything new.
  • Rough elbows and knees: an occasional gentle scrub with an Italy towel, then cream.
  • Shared bathroom at home: one large bottle the whole family can use.

A shower routine that helps

  • Keep it short. The AAD suggests 5 to 10 minutes.
  • Use warm water, not hot.
  • Go light on cleanser. Use a mild one, only where you need it.
  • Moisturize within minutes, while your skin is still damp.

Where to shop

  • Korean beauty stores and drugstores carry the ILLIYOON line in several sizes. Compare sizes before you buy.
  • Amorepacific Mall is the brand group’s own online store, useful for checking sizes and current listings.

A weekly rhythm

  • Every day: a short warm shower, mild wash, cream on damp skin.
  • Once a week, at most: a long soak, a gentle pass with an Italy towel, then a generous layer of cream. Seoul bathhouse scrub attendants told CNN Travel that scrubbing more often risks over-exfoliation.
  • If skin stings or turns red: skip the scrub and keep the cream.

Best for Dry, Sensitive Skin: ILLIYOON Ceramide Ato Concentrate Cream

ILLIYOON
Ceramide Ato Concentrate Cream
Not sold in the US. Where to buy: Amorepacific Mall (online, Korea) and Korean beauty stores

Size: 230 ml at Amorepacific Mall (checked Nov. 16, 2026). Other sizes exist, so check the tube.

Why it's worth it: A thick cream from ILLIYOON, an Amorepacific brand, labeled for sensitive and dry skin. The tube reads "Fragrance Free," "9 FREE" and vegan, with soy-ceramide and madecassoside called out on the front. The big tube makes sense for elbows, shins and hands, where body skin often feels driest.

Who it's for: Anyone with dry or easily irritated body skin, and anyone avoiding added fragrance. If you have a skin condition, ask a dermatologist first.

How to use it: After a short, warm shower, smooth it onto skin that is still damp. Reapply on rough spots whenever they feel tight.

Listed in the National Eczema Association's Seal of Acceptance product directory (checked Nov. 2026).

More to know
  • Key ingredients: Soy-ceramide and madecassoside (front label). The NEA directory listing also names ceramide NP, cholesterol, squalane and glycerin.
  • Where to buy: Amorepacific Mall (online, Korea) and Korean beauty stores

Two more from the same shelf

  • ILLIYOON Ceramide Ato Lotion: the lighter, pump-bottle partner to the cream. Amorepacific Mall lists it under body care in 564 ml bottles and showed it at No. 3 in its weekly body care ranking when we checked (Nov. 16, 2026).
  • ILLIYOON Ceramide Ato 6.0 Top to Toe Wash: a wash named for head-to-toe use, sold in 500 ml and 1,000 ml sizes at Amorepacific Mall. Read the label for where to use it.
  • Italy towel (이태리타월): the green scrub mitt Korean bathhouses use. Light and easy to pack. Read our Italy towel guide before you use it.

Words on the label

  • 바디워시 (ba-di-wo-si): body wash.
  • 바디로션 (ba-di-ro-syeon): body lotion.
  • 바디크림 (ba-di-keu-rim): body cream.
  • 무향 (mu-hyang): no scent. The AAD notes "unscented" products may contain masking chemicals, so look for "fragrance free" if scent bothers your skin.
  • 민감 (min-gam): sensitive.
  • 대용량 (dae-yong-ryang): large size.

Packing body care home

Carry-on limit: each liquid, cream or gel must be 3.4 oz (100 mL) or less, in one quart-size bag (as of Nov. 16, 2026. Source: TSA). Rules can change, so check before you fly.

  • Big tubs go in checked bags. Tape the caps and put each in a zip bag.
  • Leave room. Family-size bottles are heavy. Weigh your bag before the airport.

Frequently Asked Questions

What Korean body care should I buy in Seoul?

Start with one rich, fragrance-free cream, a mild wash and an Italy towel. ILLIYOON's Ceramide Ato line comes in family sizes, from tubes to big pump bottles. Skip the biggest bottles if you travel carry-on only, since containers over 100 mL go in checked bags.

Is Korean body lotion good for dry skin?

It can be, if you pick a thick, fragrance-free formula and apply it to damp skin. The American Academy of Dermatology says creams and ointments work better than thin lotions for dry skin. Check the label for "fragrance free."

Can I bring Korean body lotion on a plane to the US?

Yes, but in carry-on bags each container must be 3.4 oz (100 mL) or less, in one quart-size bag, under TSA rules. A 230 ml cream or 564 ml lotion goes in your checked bag. Tape the caps and bag them in case of leaks.
