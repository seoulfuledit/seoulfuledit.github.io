We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. Site analytics never include your name or email address.

Creator introductions to brands. If you are in our Creator Network and a brand is a good fit, we first email you the brand's name and what the collaboration involves, and ask whether we may introduce you. Only if you say yes do we send that brand your name, your channel links, your follower counts, the affiliate programs you use and the kind of content you make. We never give brands the information of creators who have not joined the network, and we are not paid for passing on personal information.

Where creator information comes from

When you apply, you give us the information yourself. When we contact you first, we collect only information you have made public as a creator: your profile and posts on Instagram, TikTok and YouTube, your public storefront or shop pages (for example, Amazon storefront, LTK or ShopMy), and creator search services that list public creator profiles. We look at these pages ourselves. We do not log in to your accounts, buy contact lists, guess email addresses, or collect information you have shared only with your followers. Our first email to you says where we found your contact details, and you can ask us at any time to delete them.

Emails we send

Promotional emails are marked as advertising, include our company details and an unsubscribe link, and are not sent between 9 pm and 8 am Korea time. We ask subscribers to confirm their consent every two years. You can unsubscribe at any time with the link in any email.

Emails to creators about the Creator Network and brand collaborations come from editor@seoulfuledit.com. Each one includes our company name and postal address and a simple way to tell us to stop. If you tell us to stop, we stop within 10 business days and do not contact you again, except to confirm your request.

Cookies and analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 to count visits and see which articles are read. It sets first-party cookies named _ga and _ga_<ID> that last up to 13 months. We do not use advertising cookies.

If you visit from Europe (we check your browser's time zone setting, which is not sent to us), we show a cookie banner, and Google Analytics does not load at all until you choose "Accept". If you choose "Reject" or do not choose, no analytics data is collected from you. You can change your choice at any time with "Cookie settings" at the bottom of every page. Your choice is stored in your browser.

Elsewhere, Google Analytics runs when you open a page. You can block or delete its cookies in your browser settings, or, on a desktop browser, install Google's free opt-out add-on (https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout). You can also turn analytics off on this site: choose "Cookie settings" at the bottom of any page and select "Reject". Google Analytics will then not load on your later visits in this browser, and its cookies are deleted. The site works the same without them.

When you click a link to a retailer (for example, Amazon), that retailer may set its own cookies under its own privacy policy.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to show, correct or delete your personal information, to tell you where we got it, or to stop using it, by emailing editor@seoulfuledit.com. Creators can leave the Creator Network at any time the same way; we then delete your application and stop all contact. Leaving does not affect information a brand already received with your agreement, but we will tell that brand you have left if you ask.

If you live in a US state with a consumer privacy law (for example, California, Colorado, Connecticut, Oregon, Texas or Virginia), you also have these rights, as your state's law provides: to know what personal information we collect, where it comes from, why we use it and to whom we disclose it, and to get a copy; to correct it; to delete it; and to opt out of its sale, "sharing" for targeted advertising, or profiling. We do not sell or share personal information or use it for profiling that has legal or similarly significant effects. We do not collect sensitive personal information as defined by those laws. You may use an authorized agent; we may ask the agent for proof that you authorized them. We will not treat you differently for using any of these rights. If we decline your request, you can appeal by replying to our answer with the word "Appeal"; we will answer within 45 days and explain our decision.

Categories of personal information we collect from creators, using California's terms: identifiers (name, email address, social media handles), professional information (channels, follower counts, affiliate programs, content type), and general location (US state). We use and disclose them only for the purposes and retention periods in the table above. In the past 12 months we have disclosed these categories only to our service provider Google and, with the creator's agreement, to brands.

We reply within 10 days.

How we delete and protect your information

When the retention period ends or you ask us to delete your information, we remove it from our mailing list and inbox so that it cannot be restored. Analytics data is deleted automatically as described above. Only company accounts can open these records, and we keep the number of people with access to a minimum. Creator Network information is kept in one spreadsheet in our company Google Drive, shared with no one outside the company. When the retention period in the table ends, or you ask, we delete your row and the matching form response.
