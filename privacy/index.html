Cookies and analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 to count visits and see which articles are read. It sets first-party cookies named _ga and _ga_<ID> that last up to 13 months. We do not use advertising cookies.

If you visit from Europe (we check your browser's time zone setting, which is not sent to us), we show a cookie banner, and Google Analytics does not load at all until you choose "Accept". If you choose "Reject" or do not choose, no analytics data is collected from you. You can change your choice at any time with "Cookie settings" at the bottom of every page. Your choice is stored in your browser.

Elsewhere, Google Analytics runs when you open a page. You can block or delete its cookies in your browser settings, or, on a desktop browser, install Google's free opt-out add-on (https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout). You can also turn analytics off on this site: choose "Cookie settings" at the bottom of any page and select "Reject". Google Analytics will then not load on your later visits in this browser, and its cookies are deleted. The site works the same without them.

When you click a link to a retailer (for example, Amazon), that retailer may set its own cookies under its own privacy policy.

If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland

We use your information on these legal bases: your consent for analytics cookies and for the newsletter; and our legitimate interests in replying to messages you send us and in keeping the site secure (hosting logs). You can withdraw consent at any time with "Cookie settings" or the unsubscribe link, without affecting processing before you withdrew. You also have the right to access, correct or delete your information, to object, to restrict processing, to data portability, and to complain to your local data protection authority.

We are based in Korea, which the European Commission and the UK have recognised as providing an adequate level of data protection. Google LLC and GitHub, Inc. participate in the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, including its UK Extension and the Swiss-U.S. framework.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to show, correct or delete your personal information, or to stop using it, by emailing us. If you live in a US state with a privacy law, such as California, you have these rights too, and we will not treat you differently for using them. We reply within 10 days.

How we delete and protect your information

When the retention period ends or you ask us to delete your information, we remove it from our mailing list and inbox so that it cannot be restored. Analytics data is deleted automatically as described above. Only company accounts can open these records, and we keep the number of people with access to a minimum.
