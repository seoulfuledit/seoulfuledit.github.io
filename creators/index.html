Who we are

The Seoulful Edit is an online magazine for US readers about Korean beauty: what Koreans actually use, how to shop for it, and which products are worth trying.

It is published by CoreBridge Co., Ltd., a Korean company that helps Korean beauty brands launch in the US. The Creator Network is where we connect those brands with US creators who already recommend products to their audience.

Any size is welcome. We care more about how your audience responds than about follower count.

Which links do you use?

When the brand runs an Amazon campaign, you join it and share the product from your own storefront. Amazon tracks the sale and pays your commission.

When the brand runs its program on LTK, you add the product to your LTK shop and earn on the sales your posts drive.

Where a brand works through ShopMy, you get its product in your ShopMy shelf and earn on every sale through it.

When the brand sells on TikTok Shop, you add the product to your showcase and earn commission on orders from your videos.

Starting out is fine. For each collaboration we tell you which platform the brand uses and how to set up your link, step by step.

Do I have to post after joining?

No. Joining only means we may contact you. Each collaboration tells you what it asks for, usually one short video and your link, and you decide whether to take part.

How is commission paid?

Through the brand’s affiliate platform, such as Amazon, LTK, ShopMy or TikTok Shop. The platform tracks your sales and pays you directly. We never ask for your bank details.

Do you need a minimum follower count?

No. We look at how your audience responds and whether your content fits the brand.

Will you share my details with brands?

Only with your agreement for each brand. We email you the brand’s name and the collaboration first, and send your profile only if you say yes.

How do I disclose a collaboration?

Every collaboration follows FTC rules. When you share a partner brand’s product, you say so clearly, for example with #ad or a paid partnership label. We show you how.

How do I leave?

Email editor@seoulfuledit.com and we delete your application and stop all contact.
